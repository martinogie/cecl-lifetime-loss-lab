## Out-of-time review — Martins Ogiekpolor, 19 November 2026

My written expectation was partly right (late loans under-predicted; vintage bar failed) and partly wrong (I expected current loans to be over-predicted; they were under-predicted at 0.52). The main weakness is the current-loan model under-predicting the roll to 30 days late (0.75), which compounds over the projection.
Decisions: (1) diagnose the failure for the next model version only; any fix is validated on a new period. (2) Continue Phases 3–4 on the @challenger models, with this result stated next to every reserve figure.
